Unit 2 • Move to the Music

Productive Vocabulary 1–25

Learn the word, pronunciation, meaning and two simple example sentences.
🎙 US English voice
Choose the US voice that sounds most natural. The page will remember the choice.
How to study: Read the word → press 🔊 → say it aloud → read both sentences → cover the word and try to spell it.
1
cello
CHEL-oh
Meaning: a large string instrument played with a bow.
1. The girl plays the cello.
2. The cello has a deep sound.
2
classical music
KLASS-i-kuhl MYOO-zik
Meaning: music written in a traditional style, often played by an orchestra.
1. My dad likes classical music.
2. We listened to classical music at school.
3
concert
KON-surt
Meaning: a live music performance.
1. We went to a concert last night.
2. The concert starts at seven o'clock.
4
dancer
DAN-ser
Meaning: a person who dances.
1. The dancer moves beautifully.
2. She wants to be a dancer.
5
drums
drumz
Meaning: a musical instrument that you hit with sticks or your hands.
1. He plays the drums loudly.
2. The drums make a strong beat.
6
flute
floot
Meaning: a long musical instrument that you blow into.
1. She can play the flute.
2. The flute has a light sound.
7
keyboard
KEE-bord
Meaning: an electronic musical instrument with black and white keys.
1. He plays songs on the keyboard.
2. The keyboard is next to the drums.
8
pop music
pop MYOO-zik
Meaning: popular modern music with a strong beat.
1. Zyler likes pop music.
2. We danced to pop music.
9
singer
SING-er
Meaning: a person who sings.
1. The singer has a beautiful voice.
2. My favourite singer is on TV.
10
violin
vy-uh-LIN
Meaning: a small string instrument played with a bow.
1. She plays the violin every day.
2. The violin is smaller than a cello.
11
art
art
Meaning: the subject of drawing, painting and making things.
1. We paint pictures in art class.
2. Art is my favourite subject.
12
backpack (US)
BACK-pack
Meaning: a bag carried on your back.
1. I put my books in my backpack.
2. His backpack is very heavy.
13
rucksack (UK)
RUCK-sack
Meaning: a bag carried on your back.
1. She carries a rucksack to school.
2. The rucksack is under the desk.
14
club
klub
Meaning: a group of people who meet for the same activity or interest.
1. I joined the music club.
2. Our school has a chess club.
15
college
KOL-ij
Meaning: a place where people study after secondary school.
1. My sister studies at college.
2. The college is near our home.
16
competition
kom-puh-TISH-un
Meaning: an event where people try to win.
1. He joined a singing competition.
2. Our team won the competition.
17
dictionary
DIK-shuh-nair-ee
Meaning: a book or app that tells you the meanings of words.
1. I use a dictionary to check new words.
2. Please look up the word in the dictionary.
18
flag
flag
Meaning: a piece of cloth with colours or a design that represents a country or group.
1. The flag is flying in the wind.
2. We drew our national flag.
19
geography
jee-OG-ruh-fee
Meaning: the study of countries, places and the Earth.
1. We learn about maps in geography.
2. Geography is interesting.
20
glue
gloo
Meaning: a sticky substance used to join things together.
1. Use glue to stick the paper together.
2. There is some glue on the desk.
21
group
groop
Meaning: a number of people or things together.
1. We worked in a group of four.
2. Our group made a poster.
22
gym
jim
Meaning: a place for exercise and sports.
1. We play basketball in the gym.
2. The gym is next to the library.
23
history
HIS-tuh-ree
Meaning: the study of things that happened in the past.
1. We learn about old buildings in history.
2. History tells us about the past.
24
language
LANG-gwij
Meaning: a system of words people use to speak and write.
1. English is a language.
2. She can speak more than one language.
25
maths (UK)
maths
Meaning: the school subject about numbers, shapes and calculations.
1. We learn fractions in maths.
2. Maths is my first lesson today.
